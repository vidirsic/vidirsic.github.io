Mapping the Universe: Precision Cosmology & LSS

Standard Rulers: BAO in BOSS & DESI

The Lyman-α forest provides a unique measurement of the Baryon Acoustic Oscillations (BAO) at high redshift. My work within major surveys like SDSS-III/BOSS and the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) focuses on using these standard rulers to constrain the expansion history of the Universe.

The 1D Power Spectrum & Early Dark Energy

Using the PRIYA simulation suite, we push the 1D flux power spectrum to its limits to measure ΛCDM parameters. Additionally, in collaboration with Goldstein et al., we explore signatures of Early Dark Energy (EDE) that could resolve current cosmological tensions.

The 3D Frontier: Euclid & WST

The transition from 1D to 3D analysis (de Belsunce et al. 2022/24) represents the next era of IGM cosmology. This work paves the way for cross-correlations between the forest and galaxy surveys in upcoming missions like Euclid and the Wide-field Spectroscopic Telescope (WST).
