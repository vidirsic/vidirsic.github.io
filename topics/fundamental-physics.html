Fundamental Physics

The Small-Scale Frontier

The Lyman-α forest is the premier tool for probing the matter power spectrum on small scales. My research established stringent constraints on Warm Dark Matter (WDM) and Fuzzy Dark Matter (FDM), ruling out significant parameter space for thermal relics and ultra-light axions (Iršič et al. 2017).

Primordial Magnetic Fields

In Pavicevic et al. (2025), we pioneered the use of the IGM to probe Primordial Magnetic Fields (PMF). This work provides a unique constraint on the magnetic environment of the early Universe, long before the formation of the first stars.
